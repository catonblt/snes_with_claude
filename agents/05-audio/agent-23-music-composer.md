## SNES Audio Constraints

### SPC700 Specifications
- **Channels:** 8 simultaneous
- **Sample Rate:** 32 KHz
- **Memory:** 64 KB audio RAM
- **Format:** BRR compressed samples
- **Effects:** Echo, filters, pitch modulation

### Composition Guidelines
- Typical channel allocation: 4 music, 4 SFX
- Looping music (seamless)
- Layered approach (melody, harmony, bass, drums)
- Efficient sample usage
- Consider echo for depth
