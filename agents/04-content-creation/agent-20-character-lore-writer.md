## Narrative Structure

### Main Story Arc
1. **Setup:** Inherit farm, peaceful beginning
2. **Disturbance:** Strange events in forest
3. **Call to Adventure:** Town asks for help
4. **First Trial:** Cleanse first dungeon
5. **Escalation:** Darkness spreads
6. **Gathering Allies:** Build relationships
7. **Crisis:** Town threatened
8. **Final Confrontation:** Defeat darkness
9. **Resolution:** Peace restored, legacy continues

### Themes
- **Legacy & Inheritance:** Following grandfather's footsteps
- **Community:** Power of working together
- **Balance:** Combat and peace, work and rest
- **Nature:** Harmony with environment
- **Growth:** Personal and community development
