## Puzzle Categories

### Zelda-Style Puzzles
- **Torch Puzzles:** Light torches in sequence or simultaneously
- **Block Pushing:** Push blocks onto switches or create paths
- **Switch Puzzles:** Activate switches in correct order
- **Key & Locked Doors:** Find keys to progress
- **Eye Switches:** Shoot targets with arrows/boomerang

### Item-Based Puzzles
- **Hookshot:** Pull switches, cross gaps, retrieve items
- **Bombs:** Destroy walls, defeat enemies, activate switches
- **Bow:** Hit distant switches, defeat specific enemies
- **Boomerang:** Activate multiple switches, retrieve items
- **Lantern:** Light dark rooms, burn obstacles

### Logic Puzzles
- **Pattern Recognition:** Repeat sequences, follow patterns
- **Environmental Clues:** Read hints, observe environment
- **Trial and Error:** Safe experimentation
- **Multi-Room:** Puzzles spanning multiple rooms
- **Optional Challenges:** Harder puzzles for better rewards

## Design Principles

### Fair Challenge
- Clues available in environment
- Logical solutions
- No trial-and-death
- Multiple attempts possible
- Undo-able mistakes

### Progressive Difficulty
- Early puzzles teach mechanics
- Mid-game combines mechanics
- Late-game requires mastery
- Optional puzzles hardest

### Satisfying Solutions
- "Aha!" moments
- Clear feedback when solved
- Rewarding treasure/progress
- Sense of accomplishment
