### 3. Progression Balance
- Tune difficulty curves
- Balance unlock timing

- Tune time requirements

### 4. Systems Balance
- Balance farming vs. combat profitability
- Adjust energy/stamina costs

- Adjust seasonal balance

## Example Tasks

### Task 1: Weapon Damage Balance
**Deliverable:** Balanced weapon progression

**Analysis:**
- Wooden Sword: 2 damage (starting)
- Each upgrade: ~40-60% increase
- Final weapon: ~12x starting damage
- Matches enemy health progression
- No single "best" weapon

**Method:**
- Calculate damage per second
- Test against all enemy types
- Ensure progression feels rewarding
- Verify endgame is still challenging

---

### Task 2: Crop Economy Balance
**Deliverable:** Balanced crop profitability

**Analysis:**
- Calculate profit per day for all crops
- Balance quick crops vs. long-term crops
- Ensure seasonal variety
- Make multiple strategies viable
- Account for quality bonuses

**Example:**
- Parsnip: 15g profit / 4 days = 3.75g/day
- Cauliflower: 95g profit / 12 days = 7.9g/day
- Balance risk vs. reward

---

### Task 3: Boss Difficulty Tuning
**Deliverable:** Fair but challenging bosses

**Tuning:**
- Boss 1: 100 HP, 2 damage
- Boss 2: 150 HP, 3 damage (player has better gear)
- Boss 3: 200 HP, 4 damage
- Test with expected player power level
- Add patterns that reward skill
- Ensure fairness (telegraphed attacks)

## Balance Principles

### Fair Challenge
- Difficulty increases gradually
- Player has tools to overcome challenges
- Skill matters more than grinding
- Multiple approaches viable
- Deaths feel fair, not cheap

### Economic Fairness
- Early game isn't too harsh
- Mid game offers growth
- Late game has money sinks
- No obviously broken strategies
- All playstyles can succeed

### Time Respect
- No excessive grinding required
- Optional content is optional
- Progress feels steady
- Time investment rewarded
- Pacing appropriate
